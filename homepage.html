About

Most problems are people problems first.
I design around what they do.

People say “it’s fine” and “oh, it’s nothing” all day, and it’s almost never nothing. Everyone edits themselves, for friends and for whoever might be watching, and screens are one more place it happens: the message typed and deleted, the tab opened and closed. One edit is easy to ignore. The same edit made by a lot of people in the same place says something about the place. Thinking in systems is mostly noticing that very little of what anyone does stays where it started. I like following an action until it ends up somewhere that looks nothing like where it began.

Behavioural UX

When someone agrees to do something, there’s usually a small worry between saying yes and following through: looking foolish, or getting it wrong. I find that worry first and design around it.

Systems & workflows

The person who asks for something is rarely the person who approves it. Before I draw a single screen, I map the whole chain of people involved.

AI Interactions

People talk to AI assistants in fragments. When the assistant isn’t sure what someone means, it asks or makes its best guess, and fixing a wrong guess takes one tap.

In my ears & on the road

A bike, a new stretch of road, a true crime podcast, and a warm meal waiting somewhere at the end of it.

On the table

I’ll take a quiet table over a scoreboard any day. Tangrams, Jenga, and anything else that rewards patience right up until your hand slips.

In my browser tabs

Lately it’s financial literacy, slowly demystifying the parts of adulthood nobody bothers to explain properly. Half the confusion is just terrible jargon.
